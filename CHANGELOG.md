# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release.

- Syncs your Vivino cellar: bottles, storage locations, rack and bin, ratings and wine style.
- Drinking window per wine: ready, can wait, hold, near past peak, past peak, unknown (AI estimate when a conversation agent is set).
- Wine and food pairing with Vivino food categories, or any dish through your Assist conversation agent.
- "Drink this one" marks a bottle as drunk and syncs it back to Vivino, with undo.
- Assist intents for wine suggestions by voice.
- Sensors per drinking status, a refresh button and diagnostics with redaction.
- Sidebar panel and Lovelace card (`custom:vivino-cellar-card`): top pick, mood chips, weather hint, cellar list with filters and sorting, light and dark themes.
- English and Dutch translations.
